# MASTER COMPREHENSIVE PHC FIELD VISIT QUESTIONNAIRE
*Goal: Complete situational awareness for adapting SmartTriage for Rural Village PHCs.*

---

## 🟢 SECTION 1: REGISTRATION & OUTPATIENT (OP)
**Target: Registration Clerk / Staff Nurse**
1. **Patient ID:** Do most patients have a physical Health Card, or do you search by Name/Phone/Aadhar?
2. **First Contact:** Does a nurse take vitals (BP/Temp) before the patient sees the doctor, or does the doctor do everything?
3. **Queue Management:** Is there a "priority" for elderly or emergency cases, or is it strictly first-come-first-served?
4. **Returning Patients:** How do you find a patient's history from 6 months ago? (Is it in a big book?)
5. **Volume:** What is the busiest day of the week? Why? (e.g., Market day, Tuesday clinics?)

## 🤰 SECTION 2: MATERNAL & CHILD HEALTH (ANC/PNC)
**Target: Staff Nurse / VHN (Village Health Nurse)**
1. **Village Mapping:** How do you know about a new pregnancy in the village? (ASHA worker report?)
2. **Tracking:** Can I see the "ANC Register"? (Observe the columns used—we should match these).
3. **High-Risk Flags:** Beyond BP and Weight, what else makes a pregnancy "High Risk" in this area? (Malnutrition, distance from hospital?)
4. **Home Deliveries:** Are there still home deliveries in the village? How do you record them?
5. **Post-Natal:** How many follow-up visits are mandatory after delivery?

## 💊 SECTION 3: PHARMACY & INVENTORY
**Target: Pharmacist**
1. **Prescription:** How do you receive orders from the doctor? (A small piece of paper, or the patient tells you?)
2. **Stock Alerts:** How do you know when a critical medicine (like Insulin or ARV) is running low?
3. **Stock Outs:** If a medicine is not available, where do you send the patient?
4. **Expiry:** How do you track batches that are about to expire?

## 🧪 SECTION 4: LABORATORY & DIAGNOSTICS
**Target: Lab Technician**
1. **Available Tests:** What are the top 5 tests you run daily? (Glucose, Hemoglobin, Urine, Malaria, Typhoid?)
2. **Manual vs Digital:** Are the results written by hand or printed from a machine?
3. **Sample Transport:** If a test can't be done here, how is the sample sent to the GH? Who carries it?

## 🚑 SECTION 5: EMERGENCY & REFERRAL (108 System)
**Target: Medical Officer / Ambulance Driver**
1. **Stabilization:** What equipment do you have to keep a patient alive until the ambulance arrives? (Oxygen, Defibrillator, IV?)
2. **Communication:** Do you call the GH to tell them a "Referral" is coming? Do they always have a bed ready?
3. **The "Golden Hour":** How long does the 108 ambulance usually take to reach this PHC after you call?
4. **Night Emergencies:** If a patient arrives at 2 AM, who is on duty? How do they call the doctor?

## ❄️ SECTION 6: COLD CHAIN & VACCINES
**Target: Cold Chain Handler**
1. **Temp Monitoring:** How do you monitor the vaccine fridge temperature? (Twice daily manual log?)
2. **Power Failure:** What is the backup plan if the power goes out for 6 hours?
3. **Waste:** How is the "Open Vial" policy handled?

## 📋 SECTION 7: OUTREACH & FIELD WORK (ASHAs/VHNs)
**Target: ASHA Workers / VHNs**
1. **Data Collection:** Do you use a mobile app in the village, or do you write in a diary and bring it to the PHC?
2. **Patient Transport:** How do you convince a hesitant villager to come to the PHC for a checkup?
3. **Screening:** Do you do BP/Sugar screening at the patient's home?

## 📊 SECTION 8: ADMIN, ACCOUNTS & REPORTING
**Target: Block Health Statistician / MO**
1. **Mandatory Reports:** Can you show me the "Daily Diary" or "Monthly Progress Report"? (Check if we can automate these).
2. **Schemes:** How do you track patients under government insurance/schemes (e.g., PMJAY)?
3. **Staff Attendance:** Is it biometric? If not, how do you track staff who are out in the field?

## ⚙️ SECTION 9: TECHNICAL & INFRASTRUCTURE
**Target: Everyone**
1. **Language:** What words do you use for "Triage," "Risk," and "Vitals" in the local language?
2. **Phone Usage:** Do you prefer using an app on your personal phone or a computer on the desk?
3. **Charging:** Are there enough power outlets for tablets/phones?
4. **Internet Blackspots:** Are there parts of the building where the signal is zero?

---

## 🔎 SECTION 10: OBSERVATION CHECKLIST (DO NOT ASK, JUST WATCH)
- [ ] **Lighting:** Is it too dark to see a screen clearly?
- [ ] **Dust/Environment:** Is it very dusty? (Affects keyboard/hardware life).
- [ ] **Patient Privacy:** Is there a private area for consultations? (Affects where screens are placed).
- [ ] **Hand Hygiene:** Where are the sinks? (Affects "Mobile vs Desktop" choice—dirty hands).
- [ ] **The "Paper Trail":** Count how many different registers/books are on the doctor's table. Each one is a feature we can build.
